Open issues

  • Arm too long for the shoulder servo. As drawn, the shoulder sees about 1.68 Nm, against 1.371 Nm in the hand calc. That is 57% of the ST3215 stall, above the 50% rule. Fix with a shorter arm or a 2:1 rope stage at the shoulder.
  • Belt stages not modelled. The 20T to 60T GT2 stage before each leg drum still needs pulleys and covers.
  • Knee bend stops near 80°. Beyond that the calf pod meets the thigh shell. A deeper squat needs a slimmer or lower calf pod. The knee and ankle are now pinned clevis joints with no overlapping parts.
  • Filament. The graphite parts and drums need about 2.2 kg of PETG-CF. The BOM buys 1 kg.
